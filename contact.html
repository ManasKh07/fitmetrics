Frequently Asked Questions

Are all calculators free?

Yes — every single one. No sign-up, no subscription, no "premium" tier. Free now, free forever.

Is my health data stored anywhere?

No. Every calculation happens entirely in your browser. The numbers you type never leave your device — I have no server storing any of it.

Can I suggest a new calculator?

Please do. Select "Suggest a Calculator" in the dropdown. I've added several tools based on requests and I look at all of them.

I found an error in a result. What should I do?

Select "Report a Bug" and tell me which calculator, what you entered, and what you expected. I take accuracy seriously and fix confirmed bugs quickly.

Get in touch via "Business Enquiry" and we can talk about it.
